Delete Your Data or Account

Enough Smoking — developed by Icebreaker Studios

There are two separate things you can request, depending on what you want. Pick the one that matches what you need.

Option 1 — Delete just your data (keep your account)

Use this if you want your information wiped but still want to be able to sign back in later with the same email.

Steps

  1. Open the Enough Smoking app.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete my data under the Privacy section.
  4. Confirm the prompt.

What gets deleted

  • Your local profile (quit date, cigarette count, price/currency, motivation text, and preferences).
  • Your saved goals and craving history log.
  • Your cloud backup, if you had signed in with Back up with email.

What is kept

Your sign-in account itself (email/password) is not removed — you're only signed out. You can sign back in, but there will be no data left on file.

Retention period

Immediate — there is no additional waiting period once you confirm.

Option 2 — Delete your account entirely

Use this if you want the sign-in account itself (your email/password credential) removed as well, not just the data.

Steps

  1. Email icebreakerstudios.app@outlook.com from the address you used to sign up.
  2. Use the subject line "Delete my account" and mention the email you signed up with.
  3. We will confirm by reply and delete your account.

What gets deleted

  • Your sign-in account (email/password credential).
  • Your local profile, goals, craving history log, and cloud backup — everything covered under Option 1 above.

What is kept

Aggregated, non-identifying usage analytics already recorded before your request may be retained by our analytics/crash-reporting providers per their own standard retention periods. This data is not linked back to you once your account is deleted.

Retention period

We process account deletion requests within 30 days of receiving your email.
