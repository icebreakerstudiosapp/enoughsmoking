Delete Your Data

Enough Smoking

You can delete the data Enough Smoking has stored about you at any time, directly from the app — no account deletion required, and no need to contact anyone.

How to request deletion

  1. Open the Enough Smoking app.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete my data under the Privacy section.
  4. Confirm the prompt.

If you no longer have the app installed and still want your data deleted, contact us at the support address listed on the app's Play Store page and we will delete it manually within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your local profile (quit date, cigarette count, price/currency, motivation text, and preferences).
  • Your saved goals and craving history log.
  • Your cloud backup (if you had signed in with Back up with email), removed immediately.

What is kept

  • Your sign-in account itself (email/password) is not deleted by this action, only signed out — you can still sign back in, but there will be no data left on file. If you'd like the account credential itself removed too, contact support.
  • Aggregated, non-identifying usage analytics already recorded before deletion may be retained by our analytics/crash-reporting providers per their own standard retention periods — this data is not linked back to you individually once your account data is deleted.

Retention period

Deletion of your profile, goals, craving log, and cloud backup happens immediately when you confirm — there is no additional waiting period.
