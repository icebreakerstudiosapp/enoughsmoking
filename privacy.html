Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This policy explains what information Enough Smoking collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

  • Quit-plan details you enter: your quit date, how much you smoked, cigarette prices, and your currency.
  • Optional profile details: sex and birth date, used only to tailor Recovery content (for example, showing the Sexual Health module that matches your sex, and personalizing life-expectancy estimates).
  • Anything you type into the "why you're quitting" field or send to the AI Coach.
  • If you create an account to back up your progress: your email address.
  • Basic app usage analytics (for example, which screens are opened) used to understand how the app is used and to fix problems.

2. Where your data lives

By default, everything above stays on your device. If you create an account (Settings > Back up with email), your quit-plan details are also stored securely in the cloud so you can restore them on a new phone.

3. How we use it

Your data is used only to run the app's features for you: showing your progress, personalizing Recovery content, and improving the app. We do not sell your personal data.

4. Third parties

We use trusted service providers to operate the app, including Firebase (Google) for cloud backup, authentication, and crash reporting, and RevenueCat for managing subscriptions. These providers process data only as needed to provide their service to us.

5. Your choices

  • You can export a copy of everything stored about you at any time from Settings > Export my data.
  • You can delete your data at any time from Settings > Delete my data. This removes your local profile and, if you're signed in, your cloud backup.
  • You can sign out of your backup account at any time without losing the data on your current device.

6. Children

This app is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone under 17.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we'll update the date above and let you know in the app.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the support contact listed on the app's store page.
